Quiet two days on the watchlist: 2 new candidates. The lit-review sprint is the real work this week, so today's digest is short.

## Top 2
1. **[WeightCode: Installing New Capabilities via Weight-Space Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25476)** · relevance 2 · persist
   A frozen LLM writes executable weight programs that install capabilities with no gradients, and the *program* (not the diff) reruns on independently trained networks keeping 83-110% of the gain. Programs stack: later ones extend earlier edits. → succession property T4 would need. Also more WSP crowding. → [[2026-li-weightcode]]
2. **[Self-Rewarded RL for Medical Explanations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24650)** · relevance 1 · intrinsic reward
   "Self-reward" here is a hand-coded heuristic (length, lexical diversity, punctuation), not the model's own judgment. Worth one line in the review as the thing T3 is *not*: an external formula wearing a self-reward hat.
