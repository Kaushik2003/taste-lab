# Scout — 2026-11-19 (first run)

## Top 3
1. **[All Quiet on the Neural Front](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10123)** · relevance 3 · grounded / valence
   Open 7–32B models can't verbally report an injected emotion state, but a probe decodes it at 98–100%, it changes behavior,
   and a LoRA learns to report it. → **Changes Q1:** use a probe readout as the valence signal, and treat self-report as a trainable add-on. → [[Models do not report injected pain (Ferrara 2026)]]
2. **[Just sort it! rank-one anti-loop edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21432)** · relevance 2 · consolidation
   The WSP loop fix acts like a constant bias along one direction, i.e. a steering vector baked into weights.
   → Proposed **Q5**: consolidate a taste steering direction with a rank-one edit, with no training loop. → [[A simple sort replaces the anti-loop edit (Fischetti 2026)]]
3. **[Training LMs for Introspective Oversight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23365)** · relevance 2 · grounded
   Trains a separate observer role to answer questions about the assistant, verified against real behavior (AUROC 0.907 on Qwen3.6-27B).
   A recipe for *training* the introspection the "grounded" test needs.

## Surprise pick
**[Richness Is Not Evenness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08892)**: under self-training, different Rényi entropies cross from loss to gain at
different temperatures. Vocabulary can shrink while collision entropy rises. A single diversity metric will mislead us about collapse, so log several orders.

## Threat watch
- WSP is exploding (8+ follow-ups in 2 weeks: VLMs, diffusion, robots, EEG, tractors). Good for citations, bad for novelty.
  Our angle has to be *taste + self-direction*, not "WSP on X".
- [WeightBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18553): frontier agents can't yet reliably patch LMs. Q4 (self-WSP) stays parked.
