# Gap check, 2026-11-23

Question from Harshit: is this a real gap? A model that reasons about its own reasoning, keeps a model of itself, edits its own weights by weight-space programming (no LoRA, no fine-tuning), and so may develop its own taste over time.

**Verdict.** The broad idea is not a gap: each part has prior art. A narrower combination is open: **repeated self-edits on an already self-edited model, kept or rejected by the model's own internal signal, with a prediction of each edit's effect before it is written.** Whether a preference of its own appears over the rounds is the long-term question.

## Point 1: edits that build on each other over time
- In the repo: [[A model rewrites its own weights (Fan 2026)]], every session starts from the base weights. Checked in v2 full text: "the model in use gets better as its sessions accumulate, each starting from the base weights: a first step toward the loop that recursive self-improvement ... asks for" ([arXiv 2611.21885v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21885v2)). SEAL (gradient, forgetting across edits), Kongsilp 2026 (gradient, rises then decays), GUARD (text edits), WeightCode (outside editor, programs stack), Irie 2022 and Nested Learning (learned rule, no judgment).
- New, no note yet: sequential knowledge editing collapses. ROME and MEMIT forget gradually, then suddenly, as edits pile up ([Gupta et al. 2024, arXiv 2401.07453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07453)). Even a single edit can trigger collapse; perplexity is proposed as a cheap warning signal ([Yang et al. 2024, arXiv 2402.09656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09656)) [authors from search snippet, check].
- Status: **open, high scoop risk.** Fan's group names recursion as the next step.

## Point 2: the model's own judgment decides which edits stay
- In the repo: Fan (own paired tests on graded answers, so the grades are external), Yuan 2024 (own verbal judgment, DPO), INTUITOR (self-certainty, gradient), GUARD (self-review gate), MAGELLAN (internal progress signal chooses what to learn). Internal good/bad signals exist (Berg 2026, Jiang 2026, Anthropic 2026) but none gates a weight edit.
- New: [Verify by Doing, arXiv 2611.15276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15276), an internal signal for output selection and updates, but tool-based and with fixed weights. Threat: [Self-Authored Verification Is Unreliable, arXiv 2607.24300](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24300), self-assigned scores stay near perfect while sealed deployment performance degrades.
- Status: **open at the activation level.** No paper found that gates self-updates with a hidden-state signal.

## Point 3: predict an edit's effect before writing it
- In the repo: WeightBench limits track at chance, Fan tests each edit by running it, MUSE predicts own task failure, MAGELLAN predicts own learning progress, Binder self-prediction follows a fine-tune.
- New: [Weight-Space Reading (Kocabay, alphaXiv, 13 Nov 2026)](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.weight-space-reading-language-models). Qwen3.8-27B predicts the behavioral effects of subtle edits to its own weights at 0.730 balanced accuracy with no forward pass (0.578 from a text description). But no conclusive self-reading advantage, and readers predicted a change for 53 to 88% of edits that did nothing. Also classic tools for predicting training effects: influence functions ([Koh and Liang 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04730); [Grosse et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03296)).
- Status: **partly taken.** Prediction from weights exists. Open: a self-model whose predictions choose or gate the model's own repeated edits, and any advantage of self over others.

## Other new finds (threats or context)
- [Keel, arXiv 2611.25257](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25257): a frontier model repairs looping in a VLM by editing its weights. Again a loop fix by an outside editor.
- [Autonomous Parameter Surgery, arXiv 2611.27920](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27920): a model implants selective backdoors by weight edits with no gradients. Safety risk of self-editing.
- [Search over Self-Edit Strategies, arXiv 2601.14532](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14532): SEAL-style, gradient based.
- [Goworek 2611.21449](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21449) (already in repo) is a replication of WSP, not the Weight-Space Reading paper.
