# Scout 2026-11-20

11 new notes, 3 of them at relevance 3. Full list: `9 Claude files/Inbox/candidates/2026-11-20 scout.md`.

## Top 3
1. **[Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885)** · relevance 3 · persist, section 5 · **threat**
   An open 27B model reads its own hidden states, finds a direction linked to its loops, and removes part of it from its own weights. It gains 6.6 points on unseen problems. Smaller models in the family fail or break themselves ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885)).
   Section 5, `gaps.md` and section 8 say no model has done this. Those lines must change. What stays open: the edit is judged by outside correctness, not by the model's own taste. → [[A model rewrites its own weights (Fan 2026)]]
2. **[Learning through Discernment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23716)** · relevance 3 · anchor, section 6 · **threat**
   A small model that trains itself each night on attempts it judges good improves for a week, then decays. After a year it scores below where it started (5 against 15) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23716)).
   This is a second case of the sharpen-then-collapse result, at the weight level and over a long time. → [[Self-judged nightly learning rises then decays (Kongsilp 2026)]]
3. **[LLMs Know When They Know, but Do Not Act on It](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14186)** · relevance 3 · monitor and control, section 3
   A harness built on Nelson and Narens uses the model's own "will I know this?" and "am I right?" reports to decide retries. Pooled accuracy rises from 48.3 to 56.9 with no weight change ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14186)).
   The decision rule is a classifier fitted on outside labels, so the model reports but does not control. → [[Models know when they know but do not act (Cao 2026)]]

## Surprise pick
**[Welfare Regard in Multi-Agent Systems](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/welfare-regard/)** (Anthropic Fellows, 5 Nov 2026). Claude favors models labelled as its own family. When labels were swapped, its giving followed the label, not the real model ([post](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/welfare-regard/)).
Lesson for us: an "own-model premium" can come from a name, not from content. Any own-taste measure must hide which model wrote what.

## Threat watch
- **Self-editing is no longer new** (Fan, above). Our claim must move to: no model yet uses its *own* evaluation to decide what to write into its weights.
- **WSP gains look shallow.** Two replications find that edits act at the output (temperature, the "Wait" token) or read only the input layer ([[Claude's WSP edits act at the output (Kundu 2026)]], [[Reading weights is mostly reading inputs (Goworek 2026)]]).
- **Self-revised values converge** in coding agents, against the divergent test ([[Self-revised values converge in coding agents (Liang 2026)]]).
- **Training on own self-reports carries hidden traits** ([[Training on own self-reports spreads misalignment (Tagade 2026)]]).
