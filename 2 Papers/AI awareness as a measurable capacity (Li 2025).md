# AI Awareness (Li et al., 2025)

**One line:** A review treating AI awareness as a measurable, functional capacity rather than a philosophy question - splitting it into metacognition (representing and reasoning about one's own cognitive state), self-awareness (identity, knowledge, limitations) and social awareness, with the double-edged framing: awareness improves reasoning and safety while raising misalignment risk.

## Claim
Awareness in AI should be studied as an empirically measurable capacity with capability and safety consequences in both directions.

## Mechanism
- Survey: taxonomy of AI awareness into metacognition, self-awareness, social awareness [unverified: full taxonomy].

## Evidence
- Review scope; no new experiments.

## Limits / what they didn't show
- Breadth over depth; the metacognition part is one branch of a wider tree, so the treatment is shallower than [[Survey of metacognition in language models (Liu 2026)]].

## Relevance to taste-lab
Section 2 positioning: the field is converging on "awareness as measurable capacity", which is the review's default assumption - worth citing to show the assumption is now mainstream. The double-edged framing (awareness helps alignment AND enables scheming) feeds the risks paragraph: a model with better self-models also fake-aligns better ([[Models fake alignment when training threatens them (Greenblatt 2024)]]).
