# Toward Artificial Metacognition (Shakarian, AAAI 2026)

**One line:** Position/survey of metacognitive AI - systems that self-monitor and regulate resource use - synthesizing cognitive-psychology concepts (monitoring, control, communication of cognition), recent AI instantiations, and human metacognitive capabilities AI has not explored. Companion to two interdisciplinary workshops; resource page metacognition.syracuse.edu.

## Claim
Metacognitive AI is an emerging, coherent research trend rooted in cognitive psychology, and its map is worth organizing ([paper](https://leibniz.syracuse.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/aaai26_metacog_eta_track.pdf)).

## Mechanism
Synthesis: psychology concepts -> metacognitive monitoring (Section 3) -> metacognitive architectures (Section 4) -> future directions; grounded in the 2023/2025 Metacognitive Prediction of AI Behavior workshops.
