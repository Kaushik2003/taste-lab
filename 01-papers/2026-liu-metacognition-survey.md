# Metacognition in LLMs: survey (Yale/UCI)

**One line:** The reference survey of LLM metacognition: taxonomy of methods and benchmarks to measure metacognitive abilities, techniques to elicit/improve/apply them, and open questions. Companion paper list at github.com/yale-nlp/LLM-Metacognition.

## Claim
Metacognition is recognized as a cornerstone of capable, transparent AI, but it is not yet clear when, how, or to what extent LLMs exhibit or can be endowed with effective metacognitive abilities. First comprehensive map of the field ([abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11881)).

## Mechanism
Survey: taxonomy of the landscape; methods and benchmarks for measurement/evaluation; elicitation and improvement techniques; applications, open questions, future directions.
