# Unified world models survey (cognitive architecture lens)

**One line:** 20-author report organizing world models by cognitive function under Cognitive Architecture Theory (memory, perception, language, reasoning, imagining, motivation, metacognition); its headline finding: motivation (especially intrinsic) and metacognition are drastically under-researched in world models. Introduces "Epistemic World Models" for scientific discovery agents.

## Claim
World-model research claims human-like capability without grounding in cognitive architecture theory. Taxonomized properly across video, embodied and epistemic world models, the holes are motivation and metacognition ([abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16592)).

## Mechanism
Survey: unified conceptual framework mapping CAT functions onto world-model work; gap identification; proposed directions from active inference and global workspace theory; Epistemic World Models as a new category for scientific-discovery agents over structured knowledge.

## Evidence
n/a (survey). The gap claim (motivation + metacognition under-researched) is their taxonomy's output - cite as such.

## Limits / what they didn't show
- Cognitive-architecture framing is coarse for activation-level work; says nothing about probes, valence directions or steering.
- "Epistemic world models" are frameworks over structured knowledge, not learned latent world models of the agent itself.
